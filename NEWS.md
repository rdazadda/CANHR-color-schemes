# canhrcolors 1.1.0

## R package

* New colors: deep teal, tundra green, warm berry, warm ivory, charcoal and
  muted gold.
* `canhrcolors()` takes several color names at once, ignores case and gives
  an error for unknown names.
* `scale_color_canhr()` and `scale_fill_canhr()` warn when a plot needs more
  colors than the palette has.
* New `scale_colour_canhr()` alias.

## Stata scheme

* Deep teal, tundra green and warm berry are series colors 13 to 15.
